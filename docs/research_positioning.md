# Research Positioning

## Existing ideas
The repository combines established technique families: sampled generation, multi-model reasoning, iterative refinement, execution-based checking, optimization, and test-time compute scaling.

## Potential research contribution
The testable contribution is not the claim that many models are inherently better. The research question is whether structured additional test-time computation improves objective task success under matched budgets, and which component causes any observed change.

Novelty and superiority are not established until a literature review and controlled experiments are completed.
