# Research Positioning

## Existing ideas used
- Multiple sampled generations.
- Multi-model proposal/critique patterns.
- Iterative self-refinement.
- Execution-based candidate filtering.
- Test-time compute scaling.
- Structured problem views.

These are established technique families; their use here does not itself prove novelty.

## Engineering contribution
The repository integrates planning, generation, optimization, compilation, testing, debugging, artifact retention, experiment configuration, and machine-readable result capture.

## Potential research contribution
A controlled study may test whether model diversity, candidate exchange, and execution feedback add value beyond simpler sampling/refinement at matched inference budgets.

Open questions: does diverse planning help beyond independent samples; does execution feedback justify cost; which component drives changes; do returns diminish as compute grows?

Novelty and empirical improvement are not established yet. Literature review and controlled experiments are required.
